System Manual

⌨️ Keyboard Controls

Move: Arrow Keys or WASD. Pause/Resume: Spacebar. Modals: Escape to close.

📱 Mobile Controls

Swipe directly on the Canvas or use the large on-screen glowing D-pad below the cabinet.

🧱 Wall Physics

Classic: Grid perimeter is electric (collision = game over).
Wrap: Snake exits boundaries and reappears on the opposite edge.

⭐ Power-up Core Cells

⭐ Golden: Gives +40 bonus points (despawns in 5s).
⏳ Temporal: Slows speed by 35% for 6s.
🛡️ Shield: Grants 1 collision immunity (breaks on impact).
⚡ Multiplier: 2x scores for 7s.

🛑 Obstacles & Level Up

Obstacles are glowing electric hazard blocks. Eating 5 foods increases Level, which increments base speed slightly but awards level-up flashes!
